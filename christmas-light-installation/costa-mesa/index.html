Costa Mesa · from our own records

Christmas Light Installation in Costa Mesa

Costa Mesa has given Forever Young Lighting three homes so far. Two went up in cool white on our C9s in late 2025, and the third is a first-floor roofline we have hung with the owner’s own strands two Novembers in a row.

Roofline $400–$800 · Full front $1,000–$2,500

Only the lower roofline, the owner’s lights, 2024 and 2025

This owner found us on Thumbtack in November 2024, already had lights, and wanted just the first-floor edge done, nothing upstairs. We hung them that month and came back for them in January.

The next fall the same owner asked for the same thing again, and it went up in November 2025. A third season is booked for late November 2026, so this one is on the calendar, not done yet.

Cool white roofline and pillars, November 2025

A different owner sent a front photo and asked about the roofline and maybe the columns. Before deciding, that owner got a picture of the house with both lit, then chose both, in plain white, and that meant cool white C9s on our lights. How that picture works.

The first night the lights stayed on until morning. We reset the timer that day to run 6 to 10 in the evening, and in December the owner asked for a 5 o’clock start instead. The lights came down in early January.

Roofline, garage and a wreath, December 2025

The third home went up in early December. Cool white C9s ran along the roofline and around the garage door, with a wreath to finish the front, all on our lights. The takedown was in January.

Asked by Costa Mesa homeowners

Each question below came from someone in this city.

Can you quote the roofline only, using my own C9s?

Yes. Lights you own are hung at their own per-foot rate, which includes the install and the January removal but not upkeep during the season. Plain white on our lights comes as warm white or cool white; the two homes above both went cool. Both rates and every color.

Can the lights come on earlier in the evening?

With our lights, yes: text the hours you want and the timer gets changed. That is part of the season upkeep. How the timers work.

What if I have never had lights done and do not know the pricing?

A Costa Mesa ranch owner told us exactly that. We count the feet of edge you want lit from your photos and the satellite view, then add anything extra as its own line.

Requests that did not become jobs

We have been asked for a front roofline only, using the owner’s own C9s, on a single-story ranch. Another ranch wanted multicolor including the peaks. Older Thumbtack requests asked for bistro lights strung over a backyard pool, and for put-up and take-down on a one-story of about 1,200 square feet.

A 90-foot ranch roofline, worked out

On our lights a roofline is $9.50 a foot. That one rate buys the C9 bulbs, the timer and clips, the hanging, any repair the display needs while it is up, and the January removal. Ninety feet of eave comes to $855. Pillars, wreaths and other extras are priced one at a time from photos. Using strands you own costs less per foot and covers less, as the price page spells out.
