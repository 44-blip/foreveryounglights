Where Should a Christmas Wreath Go on a House?

This season's quotes and designs include wreaths on front doors, above garage doors, between arches and high on gables. When a customer asked us on November 7 where we would put theirs, Angel's pick was "right above the bigger garage in the middle." This guide covers the other spots that came up this season and how we help you choose.

How did we answer that customer?

Their house has two garage doors, a bigger one and a smaller one. Angel sent a picture of the house with the wreath centered above the bigger garage door, and said the smaller garage, or anywhere else they liked, would work too. They went with the first spot.

What other wreath spots came up this season?

  • The front door. Two quotes this season put the wreath on the front door. One of those customers booked their roofline plus three small arches and a door wreath, and our install time guide uses that job as its example.
  • Between two arches. On a house with two arches across the front, Angel suggested the middle point between them, with the front door as the backup.
  • High on a gable. One design for a two-story house puts the wreath just below the peak of the upper gable, centered over an arched window.

In each case the wreath is centered: on the door, above the main garage door, between the arches, or under the peak of the roof.

Lit or unlit?

Both have come up this season: a 3-foot lit wreath on one house and an unlit wreath on another. Tell us which you want when you ask for your quote.

How do we pick the spot with you?

In the garage and arches examples above, Angel sent a mockup of the house showing his suggested wreath location and offered a second spot in the same message. If you would rather have the wreath somewhere else, say so.

Can I add a wreath after my lights are up?

Yes. One customer this season decided on a wreath after their lights were already up. Angel offered to put it up on a later trip when the crew was back in the area, or after Thanksgiving if they preferred.

How is a wreath priced?

A wreath is an add-on, priced along with the rest of your quote. Our cost page shows what each package includes, and the quote guide explains what comes back to you.

What should I send for a quote?

Text two photos of the front of your house. Point out where you picture the wreath, or ask us to pick a spot for you.
