Guide

Do You Install Icicle Lights?

Yes. We hang icicle lights along the roofline in warm white or pure white, in place of the C9 bulbs. Between October 26 and November 4, three customers asked us for icicles, and these are the answers.

How do icicles look next to C9 bulbs?

C9 bulbs make a clean bulb outline along the roof edge. Icicle lights hang in short drops below the edge. The home below is one of our installs, with icicles along both its upper and lower roof edges and a wrapped tree.

What colors do icicles come in?

Warm white or pure white. Multicolor icicles are special order. If you want color along the roof, our C9 bulbs come in more colors, and the colors guide lists them.

Can I switch my roofline from C9s to icicles?

Yes. When a Laguna Hills customer asked "do you offer the icicles?", Angel answered that we do them in warm white or pure white and can price them on the roofline instead. A customer who had icicle lights on the house the year before asked us for LED icicles after getting a C9 quote, and Angel sent an updated proposal with warm white LED icicles. Ask for icicles when you request a quote, or after you get one.

How are icicles priced?

By the length of your roofline. We measure it from your photos and a view of the house from above, then send the price with icicles. For the customer who had icicles the year before, Angel measured about 84 feet that way. The $9.50 per foot rate in our cost per foot guide is for C9 bulbs, so check the icicle price on your quote.

What comes with icicles?

The same as any roofline we hang with our lights. A quote we sent a customer on November 5, with pictures of their home lit up in icicle lights, covered our lights, the install, any fixes during the season and the takedown in January in every option. A timer comes with our lights too, so they turn on and off by themselves.

What if I already own icicle lights?

We can hang yours. Customer-owned roofline lights are $6.50 per linear foot, without track. That covers installation and the January takedown, but not season-long maintenance, which covers only the lights we supply. With your own lights, you supply the timer and extension cords, and we bring the clips.

What should I send for an icicle quote?

Text two photos of the front of your house and your street address, and say you want icicles in warm white or pure white. Tell us whether the lights are ours or yours and if you want anything else lit, like a tree. Angel measures the house and usually sends the quote with a picture of your home lit up.
