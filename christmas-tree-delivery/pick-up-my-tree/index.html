Christmas tree delivery

Can You Pick Up the Christmas Tree I Chose and Set It Up?

Yes. Choose your tree at the lot, and Forever Young Lighting picks it up and brings it to your home in Orange County. We can also set it up in the room you choose and decorate it.

No tree yet? The Douglas fir we bring has a set price. See the price and order a Douglas fir.

Where should we pick it up?

Choose the pickup option in the form. Add the lot's name and when the tree will be ready, along with your town and the day you want it brought home. Tell us about how tall the tree is.

For example, you could write the pickup place in the notes, then add directions for finding your tree there. If you are still choosing it, say that.

Want help once it gets home?

Choose pickup and delivery for the trip home. With setup, we bring the tree indoors and set it up in the room you choose. With decorating, we decorate it too.

Name the room and describe the route from the street. A note such as "second floor, no elevator" helps us work out the job. Include if you have a stand and which decorations you have ready.

Before you book

We settle the stand and decorations with your quote, then text you the tree, the total and a delivery window. You say yes before anything is booked.
