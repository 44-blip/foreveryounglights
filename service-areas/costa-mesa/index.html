Homes in Costa Mesa

The parts of Costa Mesa we get asked about most are Eastside Costa Mesa, Westside Costa Mesa, Mesa Verde, Mesa del Mar and College Park. They are not one kind of house, and that is the part that matters for a quote.

Mesa del Mar is a fairly uniform early-1960s subdivision of roughly 850 one-story ranch homes with open floor plans and fireplaces on flat interior-tract lots.

Eastside and College Park are a mix of 1950s-1970s single-story ranch and Spanish Revival houses on modest lots, with a growing share of two- and three-story remodels and rebuilds replacing the original single-story footprint.

A Mesa del Mar ranch and a three-story Eastside rebuild can sit in the same city and ask for opposite plans. The ranch gives the roofline a broad, low role; the rebuild may put less line at street level and make the entry, garage or a tree more useful. We price the visible elevation in the photos rather than using the age or square footage as a shortcut.

Associations and HOAs in Costa Mesa

Costa Mesa's single-family tracts (Eastside, Westside, Mesa del Mar, College Park) are generally non-HOA; the only verified named HOAs found are attached/condo associations in the Mesa Verde area — Mesa Verde Villas HOA and Country Club Villas of Mesa Verde HOA.

Most detached tracts here do not add an HOA step. The verified associations we found are attached communities in Mesa Verde, where the boundary between a private frontage and common exterior matters. Residents of Mesa Verde Villas or Country Club Villas should send the current seasonal-lighting rule before we set the design.

What a display costs in Costa Mesa

For the mostly single-story tracts, a clear eave line may stay within the $400–$800 Basic Roofline range. A taller rebuild or a design that includes the entry, garage, trees and pathway is considered in the $1,000–$2,500 Premium Full Front range. Both bands include the equipment, installation, season maintenance, takedown and storage.

Start with the Costa Mesa street view

Send the full elevation from across the street, then an angle that shows roof height and material. On a remodel or rebuild, include every upper edge you want considered; in an attached Mesa Verde community, include the association rule too. We answer with a defined package and quote. The crew installs through December 10, handles calls during the season, then takes the display down in January.

Costa Mesa lighting questions

Can you quote a taller Eastside rebuild from photos?

Yes, if the photos show the whole elevation. Send one straight-on view and one angle that makes the upper stories and roof edges clear, and we will say what fits inside the published bands.

My Mesa del Mar house is one story. Do I need the full-front package?

Not automatically. A broad ranch roof can make a complete display with C9s alone. Premium is for adding the entry, garage, trees, bushes or pathway, not simply for living in a particular tract.
