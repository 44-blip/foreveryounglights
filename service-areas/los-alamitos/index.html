Homes in Los Alamitos

The parts of Los Alamitos we get asked about most are Old Town West, College Park North, Rossmoor Highlands and Carrier Row. They are not one kind of house, and that is the part that matters for a quote.

Carrier Row on the city's east side was built as three separate infill subdivisions between 1947 and 1955, with streets named for WWII aircraft carriers; Old Town West and College Park North are mostly single-story homes from the 1940s to 1960s.

Roofs are predominantly low-pitched composition shingle typical of postwar tract construction, on a flat street grid with modest infill-size lots rather than large parcels.

That is what decides the number. A long, low, street-facing roofline reads from the pavement and a Basic Roofline job can be the whole display. A tall narrow frontage, a steep pitch or a tile edge is more work for less visible line, and the entry, the garage and a wrapped tree start carrying more of it. Send two photos and we will tell you which one your house is, including when the cheaper package is enough.

Associations and HOAs in Los Alamitos

No mandatory HOA was verified for any neighborhood inside Los Alamitos city limits — the housing stock predates the common HOA era. A College Park East Neighborhood Association exists but governs the similarly named area across the border in Seal Beach.

Where there is an association, it usually has something to say about seasonal lighting: the dates it may be up, the colours allowed, whether a timer is required, whether anything may attach to a shared wall or a common-area tree. We are not going to claim we know your association's rulebook by heart. Send us what yours says and the design gets built inside it. If what you want is not allowed, you hear that before you pay anything.

What a display costs in Los Alamitos

Two packages, two published bands, the same in every city we cover: $400–$800 for the Basic Roofline and $1,000–$2,500 for the Premium Full Front. Both include the lights, the timers, the roof-safe clips, the install, maintenance all season and January takedown and storage.

We do not quote by the foot, here or anywhere. Two houses with the same footage can be a completely different day's work depending on height, pitch and what the roof edge is made of. The honest way to price it is to look at what the street actually sees.

How booking works in Los Alamitos

  1. Text two photos of the front of the house — one straight on, one from an angle — and say whether you want the roofline or the full front.
  2. You get a real number back inside one of the bands, with what is included.
  3. We install from early October to December 10. Tell us your neighbourhood when you book, along with anything your association requires.
  4. Maintenance runs all season, then January takedown. If a bulb goes out before then, call and we come back out — that holds in Los Alamitos the same as it does at home in Laguna Hills.

The calendar opens in September and early-November dates go first.
