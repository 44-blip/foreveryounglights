Homes in Fullerton

The parts of Fullerton we get asked about most are Sunny Hills, Raymond Hills, Golden Hills and Amerige Heights. They are not one kind of house, and that is the part that matters for a quote.

Sunny Hills and Raymond Hills are established larger-lot single-family neighborhoods on tree-lined streets; Raymond Hills sits directly in the hills near Craig Regional Park with custom and semi-custom homes on hillside-positioned lots.

Amerige Heights, built from 2003, is a newer master-planned district of Spanish-style attached townhomes plus single-family homes with attached garages — a noticeably newer build era and denser layout than the older hill neighborhoods.

Sunny Hills and Raymond Hills ask us to read a larger lot, the hillside position and a custom or semi-custom elevation. Amerige Heights asks a different question: how much of a newer, denser frontage is detached and visible from the street. The straight-on photo establishes the effect; the angled one shows the working height and roof planes behind it.

Associations and HOAs in Fullerton

Amerige Heights has a verified named HOA with its own published boundary map. No named HOA could be verified for Sunny Hills or Raymond Hills — those read as established individual-lot neighborhoods rather than HOA-governed tracts.

Amerige Heights has a verified HOA and published boundary map, so homeowners there should send the current seasonal-lighting rule. We found no named association for Sunny Hills or Raymond Hills and will not invent one. If a property in either neighborhood has private restrictions, its own paperwork is the source we follow.

Questions from Fullerton

Does a Raymond Hills slope affect the quote?

It can affect access and how much roofline the street actually sees. Include the slope and the whole elevation in the angled photo so the quote reflects the real setup.

Do you know Amerige Heights lighting rules?

We know it is an HOA community, but we do not claim to hold its current rulebook. Send the association's current guidance and the plan will be built inside it.
