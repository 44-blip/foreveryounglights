How far we go

All of Orange County, out from our base in Laguna Hills. Maintenance is part of both packages everywhere on that list: if a bulb goes out in the middle of December we come back and fix it, whether you are ten minutes away in Laguna Niguel or up in Yorba Linda.

From Laguna Hills, San Clemente is straight down the 5 and Yorba Linda is the long run the other way, up through the 241. Going west you are at the coast in Laguna Beach through the canyon; going north you are into the flat tract grid of Garden Grove and Westminster. Everything else sits in between, and several of these cities — Laguna Woods, Aliso Viejo, Laguna Niguel, Mission Viejo, Lake Forest — are the next street over from us.

Installs run early October through December 10 and takedown is in January. Both packages are the same price wherever you are on this list — $400–$800 for the Basic Roofline, $1,000–$2,500 for the Premium Full Front — because the drive is not what moves the price. Roofline length, stories and how much you want lit are.

Cities we do not cover

Anaheim, Costa Mesa, Huntington Beach and Newport Beach are outside our area. An older version of this site listed them; it should not have. They are far enough from Laguna Hills that we could not get back out to fix a bulb in December the way we do here, and a maintenance promise we cannot keep is worse than no page at all.

If you are up that way, text us anyway. We will tell you straight whether we can do it rather than booking you and hoping.
